The AI Systems Checklist
for Small Businesses

How to use this: go row by row and tick only the boxes you can honestly say yes, that happens every time, without anyone remembering to do it. The boxes you leave empty are where your business is leaking time, leads, or money — and the row with the most empty boxes is the first system you should automate.

1 Leads

  • Every inquiry gets a response in under 5 minutes, including nights and weekends
  • A missed call automatically triggers a text back
  • I know exactly how many inbound inquiries came in last week

2 Follow-up

  • Every quote or proposal gets at least five follow-up touches
  • Follow-up happens whether or not someone remembers to do it
  • The sequence stops on its own the moment they reply

3 Scheduling

  • Customers can book without any back-and-forth messaging
  • Every appointment gets a confirmation and a reminder
  • Cancellations get refilled from a waitlist automatically

4 Reviews

  • Every completed job triggers a review request
  • Unhappy customers reach me before they reach Google
  • New reviews get a reply within 48 hours

5 Reactivation

  • I know who hasn't bought from us in the last 12 months
  • That list gets a real offer at least once a quarter
  • Replies to those campaigns get handled the same day

6 Admin

  • Nobody on my team does copy-paste data entry
  • Routine paperwork is drafted before a human touches it
  • The same customer question gets the same answer every time

7 Customer service

  • Customers can get a real answer outside business hours
  • Nothing sits unanswered for more than one business day
  • Every conversation with a customer lives in one place

8 Reporting

  • I can see lead-to-booked conversion without building a spreadsheet
  • I know my cost per booked job by channel
  • Last week's numbers are on my screen Monday morning
Reading your score. 0–4 empty boxes: you're running on systems — tune the weakest row. 5–12 empty: pick the single row with the most empties and automate that one first; don't touch the others yet. 13+ empty: your business is running on people remembering things. Start with Leads — it pays for the rest.
